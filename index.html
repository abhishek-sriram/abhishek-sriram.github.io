Most AI projects fail not because of the model, but because they never make it to production.

I'm an AI Engineer with hands-on experience across the full lifecycle: data engineering, model development, deployment, monitoring and optimization. That work spans Generative AI, LLMs, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, NLP, classical Machine Learning, Agentic AI and MLOps—Python, PyTorch, TensorFlow, LangChain, LangGraph, MCP, FastAPI, Docker and Kubernetes—to turn research into something a real user can rely on.

Based in Bengaluru, India

Focus: LLMs, RAG, Agentic AI

Open to: AI, ML and GenAI Engineer roles

Currently building multimodal LLM-powered agents at Infobell IT, after shipping semantic search, multilingual chatbots and stress-detection systems across three prior AI/ML internships. I care about retrieval quality, latency, observability, and privacy as much as the model itself.
